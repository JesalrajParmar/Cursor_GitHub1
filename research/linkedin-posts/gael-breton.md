# Gael Breton

## About

Co-founder of Authority Hacker and System Stack. Gael Breton is known for his expertise in SEO, content marketing, AI-powered workflows, and online business growth. He frequently shares practical experiments and frameworks on how AI is reshaping content creation and search.

## LinkedIn Post 1

Title:
If you think your company needs AI automation, you're probably wrong

Link:
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gael-breton_if-you-think-your-company-needs-ai-automation-activity-7359124288890617856-LNKX

Summary:
Gael argues that many companies rush into AI automation before establishing repeatable workflows. He shares an example of a content agency where workflow improvements delivered greater benefits than simply adding AI tools.

Key Insight:
Successful AI content production starts with well-defined processes. AI should enhance workflows, not replace them.
