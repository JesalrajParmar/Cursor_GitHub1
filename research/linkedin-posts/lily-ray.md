## LinkedIn Post 1

Topic:
Risks of AI Content Scaling

Summary:
Lily shared research tracking more than 220 websites using AI-content generation platforms. Many sites initially gained traffic but later experienced significant declines in search visibility.

Key Insight:
AI content can help workflows, but large-scale automated publishing often creates long-term SEO risks.
