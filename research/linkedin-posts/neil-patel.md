## LinkedIn Post 1

Topic:
SEO vs GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

Summary:
Neil Patel explains that GEO is not replacing SEO. AI search systems such as ChatGPT and AI Overviews still rely heavily on content that is discoverable through traditional SEO practices.

Key Insight:
GEO should be viewed as an additional optimization layer rather than a replacement for SEO, with GEO being a slighly inferior between the two.
