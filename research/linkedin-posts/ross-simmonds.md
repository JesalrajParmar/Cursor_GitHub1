# Ross Simmonds

## About

Founder and CEO of Foundation Marketing and Distribution.ai. Ross Simmonds is a content marketing strategist known for his expertise in content distribution, SEO, audience growth, and the integration of AI into modern marketing workflows.

## LinkedIn Post 1

Title:

Link:
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rosssimmonds_how-ai-search-is-rewiring-b2b-software-buying-activity-7454880904180662272-v3O3

Summary:
Ross explains how AI search systems evaluate brands using signals from SEO, reviews, communities, media mentions, and third-party references. He argues that AI visibility is earned through a strong digital footprint rather than isolated optimization tactics.

Key Insight:
Brands with strong trust signals across multiple platforms are more likely to be surfaced and cited by AI systems.
