## Neil Patel Findings

2. GEO and SEO should work together.
3. Brands need visibility inside AI systems.

## Lily Ray Findings

1. Large-scale AI content can create long-term SEO risks.
2. Human expertise remains important.
3. Strong brands perform better in AI search.
4. GEO should not replace traditional SEO.
5. Trust, authority, and content quality remain key ranking factors.

## Aleyda Solis Findings

1. AI search optimization should complement traditional SEO.
2. Businesses need visibility in AI-generated answers.
3. Clear entities and structured content improve AI visibility.
4. AI search measurement is becoming increasingly important.
5. Topical authority remains critical.
6. Strong SEO fundamentals still drive success.
